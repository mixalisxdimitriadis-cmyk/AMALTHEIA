WHO WE ARE

We are a yacht consultancy company, founded in February 2020, specialising in Accounting and Taxation within the yachting sector.

All partners and associates bring many years of experience in their respective fields, with over 25 years of expertise in the superyacht industry.

We apply full statutory interpretation within the yachting sector, operating with a strong commitment to professionalism and confidentiality.

WHY CHOOSE US

We understand the operational needs of the yacht, the requirements of the Captain, and the expectations of Tax, Customs, and Maritime Authorities.

We provide dedicated, confidential, and reliable shore-based support, enabling the Captain and crew to focus on their core responsibilities, while ensuring the Owner benefits from a smooth and stress-free operation.

OUR SERVICES

1

Accounting & Advisory

Bespoke accounting and bookkeeping services designed specifically for yacht structures and operations, delivered with precision and flexibility.

2

Tax & VAT Compliance

Comprehensive support covering corporate and personal taxation, VAT compliance, fuel tax matters, registrations, and ongoing advisory.

3

Fiscal Representation

Full spectrum fiscal representation (general, limited, and VAT) with tailored support for yacht operations and ownership structures.

4

Social Security & Payroll

Management of Maritime Social Security (NAT) and EFKA obligations, including payroll processing, ensuring full compliance and operational efficiency.

5

APA Management

Dedicated handling and administration of Advance Provisioning Allowance (APA), ensuring accurate tracking, allocation, and transparent reporting.

6

Financial Administration

Dedicated management of accounting workflows, payments, and reporting, ensuring clarity, control, and continuity.
